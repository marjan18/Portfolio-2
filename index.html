02 — Research

Research experience

THESIS · 2025–2026 · DIU

HealLM: An Intelligent Medical Assistant for Healthcare Query Management Based on Bengali Data

A Bengali-focused healthcare AI for multilingual medical question answering, combining LLM adaptation for a low-resource language with a Knowledge Graph and GraphRAG-based retrieval framework.
